# Visual V2 design notes

- The visual arc moves from cold corporate-night blue to a warmer personal-work decision.
- Slides 2, 4, and 8 use light backgrounds to break the previous all-dark rhythm.
- Slide 2 uses a prototype-versus-receipt still life instead of two plain boxes.
- Slide 3 treats the bootcamp as a discovered learning route, not a promotional banner.
- Slide 4 shows the bootcamp as the first station on a longer route.
- Slide 5 uses two skewed lanes to make the choice feel directional.
- Slide 6 replaces a generic grid with an interest system orbiting the work the founder wanted to create.
- Slides 7 and 8 use the protagonist only for the lived question and the quiet decision.
- Existing project-bound scene assets were reused; no new image-generation call was made for this revision.
